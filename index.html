About Me

I am a Master's student at the Beijing Institute of Technology (BIT). My research primarily focuses on Multimodal Medical Artificial Intelligence and Medical Image Processing. I am particularly interested in developing and applying contrastive learning and advanced image segmentation techniques to complex medical datasets to improve diagnostic accuracy and efficiency.

Alongside my theoretical research, I am deeply involved in engineering robust AI systems, utilizing tools like Weights & Biases for large-scale model evaluation and Agent development. My long-term academic goal is to publish impactful research in top-tier conferences/journals and pursue a Ph.D. degree in related fields.

Research Interests:

  • Multimodal AI: Medical data analysis, feature fusion, and representation learning.
  • Medical Image Processing: Image segmentation algorithms, unsupervised/semi-supervised learning via contrastive learning.
  • Time Series Forecasting: Deep learning modeling for complex sequence data.
